Major Battles of Maharana Pratap

Battle of Haldighati (1576)

The most famous battle where Pratap fought against Mughal forces led by Man Singh I. The battle took place on June 18, 1576, beyond a narrow mountain pass at Haldighati near Gogunda. Pratap fielded about 3,000 cavalry and 400 Bhil archers, while Man Singh commanded around 10,000 men.

After a fierce battle lasting over three hours, Pratap was wounded and retreated to the hills. He managed to escape thanks to the sacrifice of Rawat Jhala, who drew the Mughal attack upon himself. The Mughals won but failed to capture Pratap or his family. Haldighati was a pyrrhic victory for the Mughals, as they couldn't subdue Mewar permanently.

Post-Haldighati Conflicts

After Haldighati, Shahbaz Khan Kamboh led multiple invasions, subjugating key areas like Kumbhalgarh, Gogunda, and Udaipur. Akbar himself led campaigns in 1576, conquering Gogunda, Udaipur, and Kumbhalgarh. Pratap retreated to the hilly regions, continuing guerrilla warfare.

Mughal pressure eased after 1579 due to rebellions elsewhere. In 1584, Akbar sent Jagannath Kachhwaha, but Mewar defeated them. By 1585, Pratap recovered many areas, including 36 outposts.

Guerrilla Tactics

Pratap employed hit-and-run tactics, using the Aravalli hills for defense. His forces included Rajputs, Bhils, and other tribes. He never surrendered, maintaining Mewar's independence until his death.

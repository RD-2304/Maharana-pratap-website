Timeline of Maharana Pratap's Life

1540

Born on May 9 in Kumbhalgarh Fort, Rajasthan.

1572

Ascends the throne of Mewar after his father's death. Crowned in Gogunda.

1573

Initial conflicts with Mughals begin. Akbar sends emissaries for submission.

1576

Battle of Haldighati on June 18. Pratap fights Man Singh but retreats wounded.

1579

Mughal pressure eases due to rebellions elsewhere.

1584-1585

Recaptures many areas of Mewar, including 36 outposts.

1597

Dies on January 19 from hunting accident injuries in Chavand.
